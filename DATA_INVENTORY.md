# Operational Flow

The logistics process represented by this dataset appears to follow the sequence:

Customer

↓

Load

↓

Trip

↓

Delivery Event

Operational entities such as drivers, trucks, trailers, routes, and facilities support this workflow.

Fuel purchases, maintenance records, and safety incidents enrich the operational history.

# Temporal Field Candidates

These fields appear to represent the main operational dates and require semantic and coverage validation:

| Business event | Candidate field |
|---|---|
| Booking / scheduling | `scheduled_datetime` |
| Dispatch | `dispatch_date` |
| Pickup / loading | `load_date` |
| Delivery / completion | `actual_datetime` |
| Asset purchase | `purchase_date` |
| Maintenance | `maintenance_date` |

The fields do not yet establish one authoritative analytical calendar. The next review must measure minimum date, maximum date, missingness, invalid ordering, and grain for every candidate.

---

# Known Limitations

- The dataset is synthetic.
- Business conclusions should be interpreted as analytical demonstrations.
- Column definitions and business semantics still require evidence-backed confirmation.
- Only a subset of relationships has been tested.
- The raw CSV snapshot is intentionally absent from version control, so the current repository clone cannot reproduce the reviewed findings by itself.
- Temporal coverage and impossible event sequences have not yet been quantified.
