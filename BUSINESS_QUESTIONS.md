# Business Questions

## Purpose

This document defines the business questions that guide the Warehouse Operations Analytics project.

The goal is not to answer every possible question in the dataset.

The goal is to answer the questions that would help an operations leader make better decisions.

---

# Strategic Question

How can operational data be transformed into actionable intelligence that improves transportation performance?

---

# Customer Performance

- Which customers generate the highest revenue?
- Which customers generate the highest shipment volume?
- Which customers experience the lowest service levels?
- Are there customers with high operational cost but low revenue?

---

# Route Performance

- Which routes generate the highest revenue?
- Which routes have the highest delay rates?
- Which routes consume the most fuel?
- Which routes appear least profitable?

---

# Fleet Performance

- Which trucks travel the most miles?
- Which trucks generate the highest maintenance cost?
- Which trucks experience the most downtime?
- Which assets appear underutilized?

---

# Driver Performance

- Which drivers consistently deliver on time?
- Which drivers generate the highest revenue per trip?
- Which drivers demonstrate the best fuel efficiency?
- Which drivers are associated with more safety incidents?

---

# Operational Efficiency

- Where does detention occur most frequently?
- Which facilities experience operational bottlenecks?
- Which booking types require the most operational effort?
- Which operational processes appear inefficient?

---

# Financial Performance

- What drives transportation cost?
- How does maintenance affect profitability?
- Which customers produce the highest operating margin?
- What is the cost structure of a typical shipment?

---

# Safety

- Where do incidents occur most often?
- Are some routes associated with higher risk?
- Does vehicle age appear related to safety events?
- Is there a relationship between safety and operational performance?

---

# Future Analytical Questions

These questions are intentionally outside Version 1.

Examples:

- Can delivery delays be predicted?
- Can maintenance needs be anticipated?
- Can fuel consumption be forecast?
- Can high-risk trips be identified before departure?

These questions become relevant only after the descriptive analytics is complete.

---

# Guiding Principle

Every chart, SQL query, notebook, and model created during this project should answer one or more business questions documented here.

If a piece of analysis does not support a business question, it should be reconsidered.
